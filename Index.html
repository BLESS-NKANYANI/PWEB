About PWEB Events & Décor

Founded by four passionate friends, PWEB stands for precision, passion, and elegance in event design. We specialize in bespoke balloon artistry, birthday celebrations, romantic room setups, and luxury gift boxes. Our mission is to make high-end event decor accessible, joyful, and completely stress-free.
